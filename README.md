This is an integrated view of all interventions available in Karnataka that target Early Childhood Development,
mapped in a life-course format. Provides a clear calendar view of actions that need to be taken by intended
beneficiaries from the moment a pregnancy is confirmed till the child turns 6 years of age.

I created this to supplement the research work I undertook during my internship at the Centre for Smart Governance (Dept. of Personnel and Administrative Reforms, Government of Karnataka). 

View it live here: https://laharikv31.github.io/ecd-karnataka-interventions-timeline/
