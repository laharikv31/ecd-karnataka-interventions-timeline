Early childhood schemes in Karnataka, from pregnancy to age 6

How to read this page

Schemes are arranged by the stage of your child: pregnancy and after birth, 0-3 years, and 3-6 years. Each small card is one scheme. Colour shows the type of scheme. Tap a card to see what you can do, who delivers it, and the figures behind it. Use the top bar to switch between the step-by-step timeline, the action calendar (what to do and when, as a line you follow downwards), and the gaps view (what is missing).
Figures are as of the dates in the source links; compiled from public information; not an official record.
